## Project summary

Learn Lens-AI is an AI-powered learning assistant designed to help students understand academic concepts, get support with their studies, and make learning more accessible and personalized. It aims to make studying more interactive and efficient by helping learners clarify doubts and engage with educational material.

## Short answers

### 1. What made you choose this problem?

Students often struggle to understand difficult concepts when classroom explanations or traditional study materials do not match their individual learning needs. Finding reliable explanations and getting timely help can also be challenging, especially when students are studying independently. We chose this problem because we believe AI can make learning more accessible, interactive, and personalized. With Learn Lens-AI, we want to help students spend less time feeling stuck and more time building genuine understanding.

### 2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?

Learn Lens-AI is designed primarily for students who want additional academic support while studying. During a normal week, a student could use it to clarify a difficult topic after class, get help understanding study material, and revise concepts while preparing for assignments or examinations. It can complement classroom teaching by giving learners another way to approach questions at their own pace. Our goal is to make it easier for students to study independently and approach challenging topics with greater confidence.

### 3. If you had another month, what would you build next?

With another month, we would focus on improving the accuracy and reliability of the AI-generated explanations and expanding the range of learning materials the platform can support. We would also work on personalized learning paths that adapt to a student's progress, strengths, and areas that need more practice. Another priority would be adding progress tracking and practice questions to help students identify what they understand and what they should revise. Finally, we would test the platform with more students, gather feedback, and use their experiences to improve usability and learning outcomes.
